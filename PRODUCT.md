# Along
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
A conversational shopping assistant for exploring and comparing products within the platform. Voice and typed requests control the same product selection.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The local demo uses GPT-6 Astra reasoning, voice transcription and speech playback. Purchases and seller offers are simulated. Server approval requirements must remain intact. Catalog facts and their uncertainty must not be embellished. Product imagery must identify its provenance; missing photography must not be disguised as the real product.

## Brand Commitments
Conversation-first with a compact product stage. Use React Bits Circular Carousel, controlled by voice and direct interaction. Plain, useful language, clear prices and trade-offs; no generic assistant slogans or decorative dashboard framing.

## Evidence on Hand
Catalog records in catalog/data/catalog.json. Current illustrations are generic proxies and should be replaced by product-specific verified imagery or explicitly labeled factual alternatives.
