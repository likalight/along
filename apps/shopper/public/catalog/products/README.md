# Product media

Specification cards are authored from catalog/data/catalog.json and do not represent product appearance. Manufacturer photo URLs are mapped in lib/canvas/product-media.ts, verified against the catalog source pages on 2026-10-25. Photos retain manufacturer ownership, are referenced remotely, and may show a different color from the simulated offer. If a photo fails, the local specification card is shown. No claim of a licensed redistribution pack is made.
